Our State-of-the-Art Atelier

At Azza Jewels, every piece begins and finishes in our Muttrah–Darsait atelier. Our 16,000 sq ft facility brings together the artistry of master goldsmiths and the repeatable precision of advanced CNC technology—so every collection carries the same standard of finish, purity and care.

One Atelier, Two Expressions

Our atelier moves between the sensitivity of handcraft and the exactitude of CNC. Together, they allow Azza to create jewellery with a distinctive finish, from expressive handmade forms to precision-crafted gold spheres, chains and bangles.

Quality & Compliance

Every pure-gold piece undergoes a five-point QC check: assay & purity testing, official hallmarking, weight & dimension verification, alloy hardness testing, and surface-finish inspection.

Uncompromising Quality Control

Every masterpiece bearing the Azza Jewels name is more than beautiful—it reflects our commitment to purity, durability, and craftsmanship. From raw gold to finished heirloom, our multi-stage quality assurance protocol leaves nothing to chance.

  • Gold Purity: We manufacture jewellery in 18K, 21K and 22K gold. These correspond to approximately 75%, 87.5% and 91.7% gold content, respectively.
  • Quality Checks: Our team checks each piece for finish, weight and workmanship throughout the manufacturing process.
  • Purity and Hallmarking: Contact us for the purity specifications, hallmarking details and supporting documentation for your order.
  • Care and Service: Our team can advise on jewellery care and available maintenance services. Please contact us for service terms and pricing.
