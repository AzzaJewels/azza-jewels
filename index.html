Every piece at Azza Jewels is unique and meticulously handcrafted in our state-of-the-art production factory in Oman. Our Production Facility

At the heart of Azza Jewels is our state-of-the-art, 16,000 sq ft factory in Oman — where tradition meets innovation. Equipped with advanced casting, polishing, and precision machinery, our facility empowers master artisans to craft every piece with exceptional attention to detail and uncompromising quality.

Officially Certified: Made in Oman

Our facility is officially approved, certified, and distinguished with the prestigious Made in Oman mark — issued exclusively by Oman’s Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

This esteemed certification affirms that each and every product of Azza Jewels Manufacturing LLC is authentically crafted in Oman, from start to finish, upholding uncompromising standards of quality, safety, and Omani manufacturing excellence.
